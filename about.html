A welcoming, local home for low-carb life in Switzerland.

Low Carb Swiss is a practical community for people interested in low-carb, keto, animal-based and carnivore ways of eating.

The community is for experience and connection. Personal medical decisions stay with qualified clinicians.

Community boundary

Low Carb Swiss is for peer exchange and lifestyle community. It does not provide individual medical advice. People using medication, living with medical conditions, pregnancy, eating disorders or complex health histories should discuss dietary changes with a qualified clinician.
